Clip library

Every clip in a topic, one row each. Type a date or season into the table and it saves by itself.

Dates can be typed as 18 December 1944, December 1944 or 1944; leave Season on Auto to take it from the date, or pick one when only the season is known. Press Enter to save and move down to the next clip. A cue in the console brings up clips whose title, tag, theme, person, place or mood is exactly that word or phrase.

View only. Labels can be changed on the computer running the engine, so a shared link can never alter the tagging.
